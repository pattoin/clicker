The idea

A fidget you can wear out of the house.

Most fidget toys live in a pocket and get lost by Thursday. This one sits on your finger, so it's there in the standup, in the queue, on the bus. The block presses about two millimetres and comes back.

Each one is printed flat in a single overnight batch, then hand-finished: band deburred, block seated, faces cleaned with a pick.

The band is solid and comfortable at 8 mm wide. The top is a separate part in a second filament, which is why the colours can disagree with each other so happily.

Nothing is glued. If you ever want a different top, pull the old one and press a new one in.
